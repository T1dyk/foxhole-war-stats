Online and casualties

Players in Foxhole on Steam against each faction’s casualties per hour.

All players · left axis Colonial casualties/h · right Warden casualties/h · right

Steam counts everyone in the game, on every shard, without telling factions apart, so there is no per-faction online here. Casualties scale with how many soldiers a side has in the fight: when total online grows and one faction’s losses grow with it, that side most likely gained the players. Treat it as a rough hint. Casualties per hour are the change in total casualties between two snapshots, divided by the time between them.
